### 🛠️ What I'm working on

#### 🦵 WebcamFBT — full body tracking from a single webcam *(in development, private)*
Full body tracking for SteamVR without buying trackers. A webcam is enough.
- **Pose engine (Python):** MediaPipe Pose, anchored to the Quest 3 headset and calibrated with one key press.
- **Smoothing:** filtering tuned per axis, pinning of planted feet, and saved calibration.
- **SteamVR driver (C++ / OpenVR):** exposes up to 8 virtual Vive trackers and blends the 20 Hz webcam poses up to the headset's 72–120 Hz.
- **Desktop app (WPF / .NET 10):** Turkish and English UI with live preview, status readouts and driver install.
- **Built for:** SteamVR games such as VRChat and Minecraft (Vivecraft).

#### 🎵 Beat Saber auto-mapper + OnuradaLyrics mod
A pipeline that turns a song into a full Beat Saber level, plus a mod that turns the level into a show.
- **Mapper (Python):** separates vocals and instruments (Demucs), transcribes and aligns lyrics (Whisper), finds choruses and drops, then generates notes and a light show. 40+ "Onurada" maps so far.
- **OnuradaLyrics (C# / BepInEx):** in-game karaoke lyrics, a video billboard, intro and outro scenes, particle titles, combo and full-combo effects, and six-colour lighting.
